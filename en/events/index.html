What are our events about?

For school groups

A special dubbing class where children can try themselves behind the microphone, plus a short guided tour of the legendary Pannonia Studio building.

Team building for companies

What kind of voice actor is your colleague? Which role would you give your boss — the hero or the villain? Come to us for a team-building day, go behind the scenes at Pannonia Studio, and try yourselves as voice actors!

For film fans

Do you know where Mulder and Scully first spoke Hungarian? Have you ever been inside the building where Cat City (Macskafogó) was made? Curious about what happens behind the scenes? At our events you can not only look into the daily work of a film studio — you can also find out what kind of voice actor you are!
