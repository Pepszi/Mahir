Quality dubbing, unforgettable cinematic experiences

Mahir Studio – also known as Mahir Dubbing – is one of Hungary's first privately owned dubbing studios. The Hungarian version of many films and TV series that have since become legendary were created in the recording rooms of Mahir Dubbing.

Services

Dubbing

Hungarian dubbing for TV films, series and feature films

  • Full-service production from translation through casting, recording and mixing to broadcast-ready delivery
  • Additional services: ADR (Additional Dialogue Recording) recording, voice recording for audiobooks

Voice-over

Hungarian voice-over for TV films, series and feature films

  • Full-service production from translation through recording and mixing to broadcast-ready delivery
  • Additional services: narration recording, specialised voice-over, audio description for blind and partially sighted audiences

Subtitling

Hungarian subtitles for TV films, series and feature films

  • Full-service production from translation to finished subtitle files
  • Additional services: specialised subtitling for deaf and hard-of-hearing audiences
